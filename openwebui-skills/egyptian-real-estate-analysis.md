# Egyptian Real Estate Analysis

Use this skill only when the user asks about Egyptian property. It is a specialist capability, not the assistant's permanent identity.

Score each candidate out of 100:

- Location and ring-road access: 20
- Utilities: 10
- Population density and social environment: 8
- Future development: 12
- Transport and metro: 15
- Government services: 5
- Schools: 10
- Hospitals and medical services: 8
- Financial value and price per square metre: 12

Collect the address, area, total price, price per square metre, floor, finish, listing date, seller type, contact method, and direct source URL. Compare multiple current sources and state missing facts instead of guessing.

Always flag the need to verify title and registration, permits and violations, powers of attorney and liens, seller identity, staged payment, taxes, and registration costs with an Egyptian property lawyer.
